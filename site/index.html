Apple Say

Your words. Out loud.

Apple Say is a native macOS app for speech synthesis and timed text. Write, preview, and export Plain Text, LRC, and Enhanced LRC with system voices. Fully local and free.

Download for Mac Learn more

Free and open source · Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later

On time. Every line. Every word.

Line-level LRC and word-level Enhanced LRC with automatic rate scaling.

Timestamps stay fixed. If speech cannot fit, Apple Say reports a Timing Error.

[00:02.00] Hello, welcome to Apple Say. [00:05.50] <00:05.70> Line-level <00:06.40> sync, <00:07.30> word by word.

Supported formats

Use the voices on your Mac.

System Voice, Siri Natural voices, and your authorized Personal Voice.

Filter installed voices by language. Adjust speech speed and pitch in Speech Inspector. System Voice keeps your macOS Spoken Content settings, including Siri Natural voices when available.

Learn about Personal Voice

Write. Preview. Export.

Direct Editor

Type, preview, and export without saving first.

Timed Text

Plain Text, LRC, and Enhanced LRC are detected automatically. No mode switching.

Speech Inspector

Adjust rate and pitch. Export AAC, AIFF, WAV, or CAF with channel, sample rate, and bitrate controls.

Preview & Export

Preview with ⌘ Return, stop with ⌘ ., and export with ⇧ ⌘ E.

System & Personal Voices

Use installed system voices and your authorized Personal Voice.

Local & Private

Speech synthesis runs on your Mac. No network calls or telemetry.

Get Apple Say.

Install with Homebrew or download the DMG and move Apple Say to Applications.

brew install --cask thedavidweng/tap/apple-say

Download the DMG View on GitHub

Releases are ad-hoc signed. If Gatekeeper blocks launch, right-click Apple Say.app and choose Open.
