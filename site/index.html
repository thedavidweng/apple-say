Apple Say

Your words. Out loud.

Apple Say is a native macOS studio for speech synthesis and timed text. Write, preview, and export Plain Text, LRC, and Enhanced LRC with system voices — fully local, offline, and subscription-free.

Download for Mac Learn more

Free and open source · Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later

On time. Every line. Every word.

Line-level LRC and word-level Enhanced LRC, spoken in sync with automatic rate scaling.

Timestamps are honored, never moved. When speech cannot fit the timing, Apple Say reports a Timing Error instead of rewriting your text.

[00:02.00] Hello, welcome to Apple Say. [00:05.50] <00:05.70> Line-level <00:06.40> sync, <00:07.30> word by word.

More about supported formats

Every voice your Mac already has.

System Voice, Siri Natural voices, and your authorized Personal Voice.

Filter installed macOS voices by language, then tune speech speed and pitch in the Speech Inspector. Selecting System Voice preserves your Spoken Content settings — including Siri Natural voices where macOS exposes them.

Learn about Personal Voice

Everything you need to make text speak.

Direct Editor

Start typing immediately. Preview and export audio without saving a file first.

Timed Text

Plain Text, LRC, and Enhanced LRC are detected automatically — no mode switching.

Speech Inspector

Rate, pitch, and export formats — AAC, AIFF, WAV, CAF — with channels, sample rate, and bitrate control.

Preview & Export

⌘ Return to preview, ⌘ . to stop, ⇧ ⌘ E to export through the standard save panel.

System & Personal Voices

Use any voice macOS makes available to say, including your authorized Personal Voice.

Local & Private

On-device synthesis with zero network calls, telemetry, or external runtime dependencies.

Get Apple Say.

Install with Homebrew, or download the DMG from GitHub Releases and drag Apple Say into Applications.

brew install --cask thedavidweng/tap/apple-say

Download the DMG View on GitHub

Releases are ad-hoc signed. If Gatekeeper blocks launch, right-click Apple Say.app and choose Open.
