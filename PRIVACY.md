# Apple Say Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Apple Say does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data. Speech synthesis,
document editing, audio export, and voice discovery happen locally on your Mac using
macOS system services.

Documents and exported audio remain in locations you choose. Apple Say has no
analytics, advertising, account system, cloud service, or network-based speech
service.

If you choose to use Personal Voice, macOS manages permission and the Personal Voice
itself. Apple Say uses that permission only to preview or export text you ask it to
speak. When native Personal Voice file output is unavailable, Apple Say may capture
audio from that selected speech process to create the requested export. It does not
record the microphone or unrelated system audio.

For privacy questions or support, open an issue at
<https://github.com/thedavidweng/apple-say/issues>.
